    Sources & method

    Everything here comes from public web sources gathered on — Milford's own New Zealand and Australian team pages, public LinkedIn profile listings, and trade press including BusinessDesk, Good Returns, Investment News NZ, Money Management, InvestorDaily and NBR. Each profile carries its own source list.

    How claims are labelled

    EvidencedStated on a public page — a Milford bio, a company announcement, or a news report.
    Part evidenced, part inferredThe role is public; some listed duties are the ordinary scope of that role rather than a published fact.
    InferredA reasoned assumption from title, remit and industry norms. Treat as a hypothesis, not a fact.

    Reporting lines

    Milford does not publish an org chart. Board composition and the Chief Executive's accountability to the Board are structural facts. Everything below that is inferred: group executives are shown reporting to the CEO because they are grouped as “Group Executives” on Milford's site; the deputy CIOs and the three 2026 investment heads are shown under the CIO because Wayne Gentle announced their appointments in his capacity as CIO. Placement of individual portfolio managers, analysts and client-facing staff is inference from their published remit.

    Skills and toolsets

    The “likely skills & toolset” on each profile is inference throughout, drawn from the person's published background and the normal requirements of their role. It is not a claim about what any individual actually uses day to day.

    Photographs

    Profile images are generated monograms in Milford's brand palette, not photographs. LinkedIn's member photos are served from an authenticated CDN that blocks hotlinking and prohibits scraping, and the environment this page was built in had no outbound access to LinkedIn or to milfordasset.com. To use real photographs, drop image files named <slug>.jpg into assets/photos/ and run node tools/photos.js — anyone without a photograph keeps their monogram. See assets/photos/README.md.

    Brand palette

    Colours are Milford's published brand values — #E47126 (Zest), #EFA163 (Sandy brown) and #303C42 (Outer Space) — per Brandfetch's record for milfordasset.com.
